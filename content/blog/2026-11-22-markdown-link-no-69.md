An occasional post[^1] from *[The Markdown Handbook](https://md-handbook.com/)*.

Among today’s links[^2] are markdown editors[^3] MDash and Finis, also a book editor. We also look at Typen, a browser-based PDF tool that produces results in seconds. 

- [MDash](https://nightcodeapps.com/apps/markdown-editor/) is a markdown editor. Write notes, books, tasks or keep a journal, each has its own designated place in the file sidebar. There is a useful book template to get you started, covering front matter, manuscript, back matter, notes and research, characters and places, and comments. MDash offers you complete control over the look of your notes, with font, font-size, line spacing and editor width all accessible in settings. One thing that bothers me about MDash is storage. Where are all your files kept? If you select a folder for backup, you can see all your files. They are stored in the folder selected as a list of notes with their original folders discarded. The developer claims: “Documents live wherever you save them on your Mac.” But where are they stored after being imported to MDash? I am still none the wiser. Export to RTF, PDF, JPG, HTML, DOCX and EPUB. Unlocking pro adds further options. Subscribe for $9.99 a year or $1.99 a month, or buy it outright for $24.99. Available for macOS and iOS.  
- [Typen](https://typen.ink/) is a PDF tool. Drop a markdown note on its browser window, choose a style and download a paginated PDF in matter of seconds. There are four styles to choose from: minimalist, readme, editorial or readnight, a dark theme. Typen is free to use, there are no watermarks and there’s no sign-up involved. Available everywhere.
- [Finis](https://getfinis.com/) is a markdown editor that has been built with book authors in mind. Font choice is limited to serif, possibly Literata, and sans-serif, probably iA Writer Quattro, but you have a degree of control over the line height, line length and editor width. As well as the chapter and verse of your manuscript, there are separate sections for notes (characters and places) and media (images and sources). Words are counted on a per project and document basis. Exports to markdown, book PDF, EPUB, Word, manuscript PDF or HTML, with the additional option of exporting your entire library as a zipped archive. Fourteen-day free trial, then pay $34.90 once. Available for macOS.
