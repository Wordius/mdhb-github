An occasional post[^1] from *[The Markdown Handbook](https://md-handbook.com/)*.

Among today’s links[^2] are markdown editors[^3] Markify and Mark O Down, plus there’s a quick look at Tiny Little Office, a free suite of tools that’s currently in beta. 

- [Markify](https://spaquet.github.io/markify/) is a free markdown editor. It offers the rendered page and raw markdown view, with left and right sidebars. The left sidebar contains your library, the right an outline and links. There’s also an AI inspector overlay. Font choice is limited and there is no font-size adjustment. There are a bunch of useful shortcut keys to memorise in `Settings`. Type / to insert a block, such as a table, image or heading. Available for macOS.
- [Mark O Down](https://github.com/Harveyhase68/mark_o_down) is a free and open-source WYSIWYG markdown editor. You edit headings, bold text, lists, links, images, tables and badges directly, and the file on disk stays in raw markdown. Wiki links, despite the claims of the developer, don’t work for me. Even after checking the box for wiki links on opening a workspace. Nor does it offer any choice of font or the ability to change font size, although the developer said this is to be addressed in a future release. What it does support is table of contents; simply type `[[toc]]`. There’s also a folder tree; reveal/hide it with ⌘⇧B. There is full [documentation](https://predl.cc/mark_o_down/); remember to replace `Ctrl`, where mentioned, with `Command` if you are on a Mac. Available for macOS, Linux and Windows.
- [Tiny Little Office](https://tinylittleoffice.com/) is not a markdown editor, just for a change, but I thought I’d share it with you anyway. It offers a suite of office-type applications, comprising, among other items, a word processor, outliner, presentation builder, spreadsheet, drawing tool and calendar. The word processor, for instance, allows you to open and export in a range of formats, including PDF, text (TLO), data (JSON), Word (DOCX), markdown, rich text (RTF) and web page (HTML). The spreadsheet offers a similar range of options. The beauty of this is that you can paste in a Word doc and download it as a markdown file. The app remains in beta for now. Available everywhere.
