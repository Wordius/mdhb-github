### The developer says…

Longform is a plugin for Obsidian that helps you write and edit novels, screenplays and other long projects. It lets you organise a series of notes or scenes, into an ordered manuscript. It also supports single-note projects for shorter works.

### Features

They include: 

- A dedicated sidebar that collects your projects from across your vault.
- A reorderable, nestable list of scenes.
- Scene/draft/project word counts.
- Daily writing session goals with lots of options to help fit your writing style.
- A workflow-based compilation tool that can create. manuscripts from your projects.
- Support for single-scene projects so that your shorter works can use the same workflows and tooling as your longer ones.
- Plus lots of commands, modals and menu items to help you manage your work.

### Our view

Not as heavy on the sauce as other Obsidian plugins aimed at writers, such as [Word-Smith](/blog/obsidian-plugins-word-smith/). But, with 190,000 downloads in around two years, Longform must be doing something right.

If you have any existing works, you can start by adding `longform` to their front matter, or properties, and the Longform plugin will recognise them. 

Alternatively, find or create a folder in your vault in which you want to write your first novel, right-click and select `Create Longform project`. That’s it, you have made a start.
