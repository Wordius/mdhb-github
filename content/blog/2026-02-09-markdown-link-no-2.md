An occasional post from The Markdown Handbook. 

Among today’s links are an interesting take on static site generators, a new release of Netnewswire, a tutorials and workshops update for Docsify-This, the latest update from markdown editor Ulysses, and a markdown-related update for LibreOffice: 

- Kev Quirk introduces his latest project, [**Pure Blog**](https://kevquirk.com/blog/introducing-pure-blog/). He says: “[It] is a simple PHP-based blogging platform that has a lovely little CMS in the background to help you manage your content. Everything is based on Markdown, and all the content is stored in plaintext files, just like an SSG.”
- Not markdown related, but if asked the question **‘what is the greatest invention of your life?’** what would be your answer. Here’s [Om Malik’s response](https://om.co/2026/02/06/the-greatest-invention-is/).
- One of the first RSS readers, and still the best, [**Netnewswire 7.0**](https://netnewswire.blog/2026/02/06/netnewswire-for-ios.html) is out.
- Hibbitts Design reveals a [Tutorials and workshops update](https://www.hibbittsdesign.org/Docsify-This-Tutorials-Workshops-207a302816404659b77839a4ebca2528) for **Docsify-This**.
- [**Ulysses** update v39.4](https://ulysses.app/release-notes): fixes for WordPress.com accounts, widgets’ issues and the ShortCuts app on iOS.
- [**LibreOffice 26.2**](https://www.libreoffice.org/discover/new-features/) now offers the ability to import and export markdown.
