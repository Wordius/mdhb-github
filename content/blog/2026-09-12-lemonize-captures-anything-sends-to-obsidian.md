[Lemonize](https://www.getlemonize.com/) 1.3 is a free app for macOS (first 20 captures are on the house) with the option to upgrade if you’re enjoying the experience. 

It allows you to capture anything and send it to one of three destinations, [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md), [Bear](https://bear.app/) or [DEVONthink](https://www.devontechnologies.com/apps/devonthink).

With capture profiles, multiple Obsidian vaults and a revamped capture experience, Lemonize claims to remove the friction between having an idea and putting it in the right place.

In Obsidian, for instance, Lemonize adds the new note in a folder of your choice, or can be set up to add to the top or bottom of a daily note.

Lemonize costs £1.99 a month or £6.99 for a lifetime licence. It lives in your menu bar and can be enabled to launch at login.
